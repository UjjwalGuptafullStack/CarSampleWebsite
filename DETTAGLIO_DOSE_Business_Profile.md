## What They Do

The business is much more than a normal “car wash.”

### 1. Car Detailing

They position themselves around complete vehicle care rather than simple washing. Their own posts use the positioning **“Every car deserves a spa day.”**

This gives room to present detailing as a **premium service**, rather than making the website look like a generic car-wash page.

### 2. Ceramic Coating

They actively promote **ceramic coating for both cars and motorcycles**.

Their LinkedIn posts specifically mention ceramic coating as a way to protect the vehicle while maintaining its shine.

This is particularly valuable for the website because ceramic coating is a service that needs explanation:

**What it is → benefits → process → expected result → gallery → enquiry.**

### 3. Paint Protection Film (PPF)

This is one of the more premium services they advertise.

Their LinkedIn describes PPF as protection against **chips, scratches and road debris**.

PPF is also an excellent website section because customers generally want to understand:

- What PPF protects against
- Which parts of the car can be covered
- How the installation looks
- How long it lasts
- What finish it provides
- How to get a quote

### 4. Bike Detailing

They don't only work on cars.

Their own content repeatedly mentions **motorcycle detailing and ceramic coating**, including posts specifically around protecting bikes.

The website should therefore give **Cars** and **Bikes** separate sections.

### 5. Bike/Car Protection and Sanitization

They also advertise vehicle sanitization and protective treatments. Their LinkedIn activity includes posts about **sanitizing cars for a safer ride**.

### 6. Denting & Painting

Justdial categorizes the business under automobile services and lists **car denting and car painting** among the services associated with the business.

This should be treated as a secondary service until the exact scope is confirmed directly with them, rather than making it the central identity of the website.

## Why They Are a Strong Website Prospect

### Their service is extremely visual

A detailing company naturally produces:

- Before/after shots
- Glossy car photography
- PPF installation
- Ceramic coating application
- Close-ups of paint correction
- Interior detailing
- Luxury/exotic vehicles

Magicpin alone currently exposes **43 photos** associated with the listing.

That means a website can look legitimately premium without needing to invent visual assets.

### Their services require trust

A customer is not going to hand over a ₹10–30+ lakh vehicle to an unknown shop simply because its Google listing exists.

The website can establish:

**professionalism → expertise → previous work → reviews → process → easy enquiry**

### Their services are high-value enough to justify lead generation

The important thing is not necessarily online payment.

For services like PPF and ceramic coating, the website can be designed around:

**View service → See examples → Understand benefits → Request quote → WhatsApp/call**

That is more appropriate than trying to turn them into a generic e-commerce website.

## Positioning Recommendation

Do **not** market them as a “luxury detailing company” unless that positioning is confirmed with the owners.

Their public listings describe them primarily as an automotive/car-washing and care business, while their own LinkedIn activity clearly shows premium services such as PPF and ceramic coating.

The safer positioning is:

> **Premium Automotive Detailing & Protection**

This allows the site to look premium without misrepresenting the actual business.

## Important Validation Before Building

Before the website is presented as a finished commercial site, confirm these directly with DETTAGLIO DOSE:

- Exact current address
- Correct phone/WhatsApp number
- Exact service list
- Pricing or whether pricing is quote-only
- Warranty/coverage details for PPF and ceramic coating
- Service turnaround times
- Brands/materials used
- Whether they offer pickup/drop-off
- Whether they currently have a website/domain under development
- Which photos are owned/approved for commercial reuse
- Brand logo, colors, typography and preferred tone

This avoids inventing service claims and gives the final website a factual basis.

## Sales Angle for the Website Proposal

The strongest argument is not:

> “You don't have a website.”

It is:

> **“You already have strong customer reviews and visually impressive work, but there isn't a single premium destination where a new customer can understand your services, see your results, trust the business, and book directly.”**

The website should therefore function as a **conversion layer over their existing Google and social presence**, not just as an online brochure.
